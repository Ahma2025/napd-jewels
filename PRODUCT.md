# NAPD Jewels (نبض) — Product

## What it is
Online storefront for NAPD.PS (Arabic brand name: نبض), a jewelry shop in Palestine with both an online presence and a physical store. It sells imported 925 sterling silver jewelry: rings, necklaces, bracelets, earrings, and a premium Moissanite line.

## Audience
Upscale customers, mostly women, buying premium gifts or something special for themselves. They mostly arrive from Instagram (@napd.ps) on a phone. Arabic is their first language; English product naming is accepted as part of the luxury feel.

## How people buy
WhatsApp is the real sales channel. The site works as a browsable catalog: customers find a piece, then message on WhatsApp to order. "Add to Cart" exists, but most orders close over WhatsApp. Roughly 70% of revenue is online and 30% in-store.

## Purpose of the site
Make a customer trust the brand within seconds and find a piece they want. Show real stock only: out-of-stock pieces are hidden from customers (owners still see everything).

## Brand
- Colors: deep green #182B2A, deeper green #0E1B1A, gold #B08D57, light gold #D9C6A0, ivory #FAF7F1.
- Logo: NAPD monogram with "JEWELS" in spaced capitals.
- Feel: quiet luxury, cinematic, calm. Gold is an accent, never a fill for large areas.
- Product photos sit on white backgrounds (the standard for listings).

## Promises shown on the site
- 1-year warranty on every piece
- Imported sterling silver 925
- Order directly on WhatsApp

## Constraints
- Next.js on Vercel, Supabase backend; product data is edited by the owner in the admin panel.
- Prices are in Israeli shekels (₪).
- Owner prefers refinement of the existing look over replacing it.
